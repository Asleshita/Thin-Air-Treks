        Safety & contact

        Up safe, down happy

        Certified guides

        Every guide holds a mountaineering course certificate and wilderness first-aid training. One guide for every six trekkers.

        Checks at every camp

        Oxygen levels and pulse are checked morning and evening. Anyone showing signs of altitude sickness goes down with a guide.

        Turn-around time

        On summit day we set a turn-around time. If we're not at the top by then, we head down. The mountain will be there next year.

        I've never trekked before. Can I do this?

        Yes. Kedarkantha, Brahmatal and Dayara Bugyal are built for first-timers. Train for six weeks and you'll be fine.

        What if someone gets altitude sickness?

        We check everyone twice a day. Mild symptoms mean rest and water; anything more and a guide takes you down straight away. Every batch carries oxygen.

        Can I get a refund if I cancel?

        Full refund up to 30 days before the start date, 50% up to 15 days before, and a trek voucher after that.
